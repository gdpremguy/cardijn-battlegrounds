About the game

Survive endless waves of enemies in a closed arena. Every kill feeds a combo multiplier, every wave earns you a new upgrade, and every fighter brings their own ability to the fight.

  • Wave-based arena survival — enemies spawn in from all sides.
  • Bullet-firing grunts, armored, splitting and kamikaze elites.
  • Multi-phase bosses, including Mr Champion and a brick-eating mini-boss.
  • Chain kills to stack a combo multiplier. Taking a hit breaks it.
  • Dash with brief invincibility frames (and a short cooldown).
  • Pick an upgrade after every wave and spend points at Mr Kempson's Shop.
  • Dozens of unlockable fighters, each with a unique ability and passive.
  • Build your own brawler in the Character Creator.
  • Glass Cannon mode: 4x damage at the cost of having a single HP.
  • Hidden fighter to unlock, all-time stats, and multiple colour themes.
